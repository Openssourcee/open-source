$OS is a coin with an open-source website. Paint a plot, write the lore or add a feature. Free, right here.

How it works

Four moves, no account, no fee.

  1. Pick a spot

    Take an open plot from the canvas, or drag out a plot of your own on any empty space.

  2. Make something

    Paint it pixel by pixel, write a chapter of the lore, or propose code and tech for the site.

  3. Send it

    Straight from this page. Your first one goes live at once; after that a person checks each one.

  4. It goes live

    Your work joins the site with your name on it, for everyone who visits after you.
